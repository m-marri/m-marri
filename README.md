# Matteo Marri
### MSc Candidate in Chemical Physics · University of Milano-Bicocca
**Computational Materials Science · Atomistic Modelling · Interfaces**

I build automated workflows to construct, relax and analyze 2D/3D heterojunctions
across translations and twist angles, to understand how atomic-scale structure
controls interfacial stability and energetics.

## Research Interests
* **Interfacial Thermodynamics & Stability:** how registry and twist angle set interfacial energy.
* **2D/3D Heterojunctions & Moiré Systems:** mapping energy landscapes over translational and rotational degrees of freedom.
* **Statistical Mechanics of Interfaces:** linking microscopic interactions to finite-temperature properties.
* **MLIPs (MACE):** reaching system sizes and sampling that DFT cannot.
